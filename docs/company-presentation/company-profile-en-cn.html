Who We Are 关于我们

NORTON GAUSS BONZINI SARL is a Cameroonian international trade services group. Our core mission is to build economic, human and cultural bridges between Africa and Asia — with a deep focus on the Cameroon–China trade corridor. We do more than facilitate transactions: we connect markets and cultures, combining African entrepreneurial energy with Chinese industrial ingenuity.

诺顿·高斯·邦齐尼有限公司（NORTON GAUSS BONZINI SARL）是一家来自喀麦隆的国际贸易服务集团。我们的核心使命是在非洲与亚洲之间 搭建经济、人文与文化的桥梁，尤其深耕喀麦隆—中国贸易走廊。我们不只是促成交易，更连接市场与文化， 将非洲的创业活力与中国的产业实力结合起来。

We are legally established on both sides of the corridor: in Cameroon, where we serve importers across the country and the wider Central African market, and in China, where our own registered company operates an office and warehouse in Yiwu (Zhejiang), supported by a partner warehouse network in Guangzhou. For our clients, we act as their operations team in China: we find the right suppliers, secure and inspect the goods, move them by sea or air, and settle payments — end to end.

我们在贸易走廊的两端均设有合法注册的公司实体：在喀麦隆，我们服务全国及整个中部非洲市场的进口商；在中国，我们注册成立了自己的公司， 在浙江义乌设有办公室和仓库，并在广州拥有合作仓储网络。对客户而言，我们就是他们在中国的运营团队： 寻找合适的供应商、验货保障品质、通过海运或空运发货、并完成货款支付——提供端到端的一站式服务。

Bonzini Buy — Sourcing & Procurement 采购中心

Many African buyers know exactly what they want to sell — but not how to find it in China. Bonzini Buy is our purchasing center: we act as your buying representative in China, helping you find the best suppliers and the best products at the best price, even if you have never set foot in China.

许多非洲买家清楚自己想卖什么，却不知道如何在中国找到货源。Bonzini Buy 是我们的采购中心： 我们作为您在中国的采购代表，帮您以最优价格找到最好的供应商和最合适的产品——即使您从未来过中国。

Who it serves 服务对象

Merchants unfamiliar with 1688, Taobao or Alibaba; buyers facing language and trust barriers with Chinese suppliers; anyone who wants to minimize fraud and quality risks.

不熟悉 1688、淘宝或阿里巴巴的商户；因语言与信任障碍难以与中国供应商沟通的买家；以及希望规避欺诈和质量风险的所有客户。

How it connects 业务衔接

Once your order is produced and inspected, it flows directly into Bonzini Cargo for shipping, and Bonzini Trading for supplier payment — one partner across the whole chain.

订单完成生产和验货后，直接衔接 Bonzini Cargo 发货运输，并由 Bonzini Trading 完成货款支付——全链条一个合作伙伴搞定。

What we do concretely 我们的具体服务

  • Identify and vet the best suppliers against your specifications and budget 根据您的产品要求和预算，寻找并审核最合适的供应商
  • Negotiate prices, terms and lead times directly in Chinese 用中文直接与供应商谈判价格、条款和交期
  • Place orders on your behalf — always with your final approval 代您下单——每一步都以您的最终确认为准
  • Check samples and control product quality before shipment, at our warehouse 在我们的仓库核对样品、发货前严格把控产品质量
  • Manage the whole supply chain through to delivery, via Bonzini Cargo 通过 Bonzini Cargo 管理整条供应链，直至货物送达

Why it matters 价值所在

For customers who don't know China well, we remove the three biggest risks of sourcing: wrong supplier, wrong quality, wrong price. You focus on selling — we handle China.

对于不熟悉中国市场的客户，我们消除采购中最大的三个风险：选错供应商、货不对板、价格虚高。 您专注销售，中国的事交给我们。

Bonzini Cargo — Freight & Logistics 货运物流

Goods transportation is the heart of our business. Bonzini Cargo moves merchandise from China to Cameroon by sea or by air — safely, with clear tracking and no stress. From a single parcel to industrial equipment and full supply chains, we handle the entire import logistics chain.

货物运输是我们的核心业务。Bonzini Cargo 通过海运或空运将货物从中国运往喀麦隆——安全可靠、全程可追踪、省心省力。 从单件包裹到工业设备和整条供应链，我们承接全流程进口物流。

The journey of your goods 货物运输全流程

  1. Reception at our warehouse in Yiwu or our partner warehouse in Guangzhou after your purchase 您采购完成后，货物送达我们的义乌仓库或广州合作仓库
  2. Inspection, verification and consolidation (groupage) of your parcels 对货物进行检查、核对，并与其他客户货物拼箱集运
  3. Secure shipping by sea freight (economical) or air freight (fast) — your choice 安全发运：海运（经济实惠）或空运（快速高效），由您选择
  4. Regular tracking updates via WhatsApp, WeChat or our application 通过 WhatsApp、微信或我们的应用程序实时跟踪货物动态
  5. Customs clearance in Douala, handled by our team 由我们的团队在杜阿拉办理清关手续
  6. Delivery to one of our agencies or directly to your door 货物送达我们的网点自提，或直接配送上门

For companies 企业客户

Bonzini Trading — International Payments 国际支付

Bonzini Trading is a reliable gateway between African local currencies — notably the CFA Franc (XAF) — and Chinese payment channels: Alipay, WeChat Pay and Chinese bank transfers. Importers pay us locally; we pay their suppliers in RMB, in minutes.

Bonzini Trading 是非洲本地货币（尤其是中非法郎 XAF）与中国收款渠道之间的可靠桥梁，支持支付宝、微信支付和中国银行转账。 进口商在本地向我们付款，我们在几分钟内以人民币付给他们的供应商。

How a payment works 付款流程

  1. The client contacts us with the supplier's invoice; we agree on the day's exchange rate 客户将供应商发票发给我们，双方确认当日汇率
  2. The client pays locally — bank deposit or mobile money (MTN MoMo, Orange Money…) 客户在本地付款——银行存款或移动支付（MTN MoMo、Orange Money 等）
  3. After verification, we pay the Chinese supplier immediately via Alipay, WeChat Pay or bank transfer 核实后，我们立即通过支付宝、微信支付或银行转账付款给中国供应商
  4. The client receives a PDF payment receipt to share with the supplier — often in under 5 minutes 客户收到 PDF 付款凭证，可直接转发给供应商——通常全程不超过 5 分钟

Competitive & transparent 汇率优、更透明

Exchange rates better than banks and typical exchange bureaus, with total transparency on fees and rates.

汇率优于银行和一般兑换机构，费用与汇率完全公开透明。

Personal follow-up 专人跟进

A dedicated team follows every operation end to end, with years of experience in Africa–Asia financial flows.

专属团队全程跟进每笔业务，在非亚资金往来领域拥有多年经验。

Also serving Chinese companies in Africa · 同样服务在非中资企业

Chinese companies established in Africa face the reverse problem: moving money home. We manage their local payment operations and settlements back to China — paying suppliers, partners or headquarters in RMB, quickly and reliably, from African local currencies.

在非洲经营的中资企业面临相反的难题：资金如何顺畅回国。我们为其管理本地收付款业务及对华结算—— 将非洲本地货币快速、可靠地兑付为人民币，支付给国内供应商、合作伙伴或总部。
