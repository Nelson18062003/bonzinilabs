# 🇬🇧 ENGLISH

Hi [PARTNER NAME],

We are running a **full compliance audit** of our import operations, and we
need your help on the China side.

**Scope — three periods, please cover all three:**
- **Every container you have already shipped for us**, from the very first one,
  since the beginning of our cooperation. Including containers that had
  problems, and cancelled ones.
- **Every container currently in transit or already booked.**
- **Every container planned for the coming months** — even if only the volume
  and the target date are known so far.

You handled the entire China side, so most of these records exist only with
you. We cannot rebuild them from Cameroon.

## How to organise it

**One folder per container**, named with the B/L number and the container
number — for example `GGZ2867008_ECMU5839181`. For containers not yet shipped,
one folder per booking.

Everything in **PDF**. Scans are fine if every figure is readable.

**If a document does not exist for a container, write "not available" — do not
skip it silently.** A gap we know about is not a problem; a gap we discover
later is.

## What each folder must contain

**1 — Booking and shipping**
- Booking confirmation / booking note from the shipping line
- Shipping instructions you sent to the carrier
- Draft B/L and **final B/L** — and tell us clearly whether it was an
  **original B/L, a telex release, or a sea waybill**
- If original B/Ls were issued: how many, and where they are now
- **Packing list** as filed
- **Commercial invoice** as filed, and the **proforma invoice**
- **VGM declaration** and the weighbridge ticket
- **Seal number**, and the photo of the sealed doors if you have it
- Loading photos and any stuffing / tally report
- **Arrival notice**
- Cargo manifest, if you hold a copy

**2 — Dangerous goods** (phones, batteries, power banks, cosmetics, aerosols)
- Dangerous goods declaration
- **MSDS**
- The **UN number** used (for lithium batteries in equipment, normally UN3481,
  Class 9)
- Dangerous goods packaging certificate
- **Container Packing Certificate**

**3 — China export customs**
- **Export customs declaration form**, with its declaration number
- **In whose name was the export declaration filed — our company, your company,
  or a third party?** Please state this for each container.
- Name and licence number of the **customs broker** who filed it
- Customs release notice
- **Export licence or permit**, if the goods required one
- **Certificate of origin** — and which type
- China commodity inspection documents, if any applied
- **Fumigation certificate** and/or evidence of **ISPM 15 (IPPC) marking** on
  wooden pallets and crates

**4 — Cameroon compliance**
- **BESC / ECTN certificate** for each container, with its number and
  validation date
- **SGS / PECAE inspection**: the inspection request, the documents you
  submitted to SGS, the test or analysis reports, the inspection report, and
  the **Assessment Certificate**
- Any ANOR / Certificate of Conformity reference you hold
- Any other certificate required for Cameroon
- **Marine insurance certificate**, if you arranged any insurance on our cargo

**5 — Costs and payments**
- **Your invoices to us**, one per container
- **The shipping line's own freight invoice**, if you booked on our behalf
- Every China-side charge, itemised: terminal handling (THC), documentation
  fee, port charges, customs clearance fee, trucking, warehousing, fumigation,
  inspection, BESC
- **Official receipts** for any duty, tax or fee you paid to Chinese authorities
- **Proof of every payment we made to you**: bank transfer slips and receipts,
  with dates, amounts and currency
- Any credit note, refund or discount granted
- Any **outstanding balance**, in either direction

**6 — Problem files**
For any container that had a delay, a damage, a customs hold, a demurrage
charge or a claim: the **correspondence** and what was finally decided and paid.

## Five questions that are not written in any document

1. **Who obtains the BESC on our shipments — you, or someone on our side?**
   Which CNCC-accredited agent in China do you use, and what do they charge?
2. For each container, was the BESC validated **before the vessel departed**, or
   **regularised late after arrival**? If a late penalty was ever paid, send the
   receipt.
3. **Who initiates the SGS / PECAE inspection on our shipments — you, the
   supplier, or our side in Cameroon?** And who pays for it?
4. **Which of the services above do you perform yourself, and which do you
   subcontract?** Please name the subcontractors.
5. Going forward, **can you send us the BESC number and the B/L within 5 days
   of vessel departure, and the full document pack within 15 days?** The legal
   BESC deadline is 48 hours before arrival in Cameroon, and we do not want to
   keep working that close to the limit.
