### C. ⚠️ BESC / ECTN — HIGHEST PRIORITY

The **BESC** (*Bordereau Électronique de Suivi des Cargaisons*), known
internationally as the **ECTN**, is mandatory for every sea shipment to
Cameroon under Cameroonian ministerial order **n° 00557/MINT of 11 July 2006**.
**Without a valid BESC the container cannot be cleared in Douala**, and
demurrage runs at our cost.

Under Article 4 of that order, the **shipper or his agent** must obtain it and
have it validated by the CNCC — **at the port of loading**, and **no later than
48 hours before the vessel arrives** in Cameroon.

Please answer these six points clearly:

1. **On our shipments, who obtained the BESC — you, or someone on our side?**
2. **Which CNCC-accredited agent in China** do you use, and what do they charge?
3. For **each container**: the **BESC number** and the **validated certificate
   in PDF**
4. For each container: was it validated **before the vessel departed**, or
   **regularised late after arrival**?
5. If any **late-regularisation penalty** was ever paid, send the **amount and
   the official receipt** — we must record it in the audit
6. **Going forward: can you send us the BESC number together with the B/L,
   within 5 days of vessel departure?** We do not want to keep relying on the
   48-hour deadline.

### D. Inspection and Cameroon conformity
- **SGS pre-shipment inspection**: the request, the report, and the
  **Certificate of Conformity (RVC / CoC)**
- Any **ANOR / PECAE** document
- Any other inspection certificate required for Cameroon

### E. Costs and payment proof — the most important part for the audit

- **The shipping line's own freight invoice**, if you booked on our behalf
