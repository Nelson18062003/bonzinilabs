We are running a **full compliance audit** of our imports. We need **every
document you hold, for every container you have shipped for us — from the very
first one, since the beginning of our cooperation, up to today.** Including
containers that had problems, and cancelled ones.

You handled the whole China side, so most of these records exist only with you.

**Please put one folder per container**, named with the B/L number and the
container number — for example `GGZ2867008_ECMU5839181` — and put in it

- **B/L** (tell me if it was an original B/L, a telex release or a sea waybill),
  booking confirmation, packing list, commercial invoice, arrival notice, VGM
  declaration
- **Export customs declaration**, and the name of the customs broker who filed it
- **BESC / ECTN certificate**
- **Certificate of origin**, fumigation certificate or ISPM 15 evidence, and —
  for phones, batteries or power banks — the dangerous goods declaration, MSDS
  and container packing certificate
- **SGS inspection**: request, report, certificate of conformity (RVC / CoC)
- **All invoices**: yours to us, the shipping line's freight invoice, and every
  China-side charge (THC, documentation, port, customs clearance, trucking,
  storage, fumigation, inspection)
- **Proof of every payment we made to you**, and of any duty or fee you paid to
  Chinese authorities
- Any **photos** you kept of the loading and of the sealed doors

Everything in PDF. Scans are fine if the figures are readable. **If a document
does not exist for a container, just write "not available" — do not skip it
silently.** We need to know what exists and what does not.

**Three questions that are not in any document, please answer them directly:**

1. **On our shipments, who obtains the BESC — you, or someone on our side?**

2. For each container, was the BESC validated **before the vessel departed**, or
   **regularised late after arrival**? If any late penalty was paid, send the
   receipt.
3. Going forward, **can you send us the BESC number together with the B/L,
   within 5 days of departure?** The legal deadline is 48 hours before arrival
   in Cameroon, and we do not want to keep working that close to the limit.

Please share everything through a **cloud drive or WeTransfer link** — not file
by file in the chat. 🔒 **Payment proofs and bank documents: send those to me
directly in private**, not in this group.

Could you first just tell me **how many containers there are in total**? One
line is enough, today. Then the full pack by **[DATE]**.

Thank you. A complete file protects you too — once everything is on record,
nobody comes back to you with questions months later.
