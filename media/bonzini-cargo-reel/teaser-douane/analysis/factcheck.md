### Corrections to claims in the report body

- **§2.1, "193 784 F de trop" on the chair:** this is an estimate, not a real overpayment. The internal file rates it 🟠 (« effet accise à confirmer ») and nothing was charged on it. Status: WEAK, internal only.
- **§2.2, the "last resort" value:** « l'organisme retient une valeur de dernier recours » is generalised from one internal case. Write « peut retenir ». Also, a tripled value triples only the taxes based on value, not « toutes les taxes ». The +115 % figure is right, but it is a share of customs value, not exactly of the price paid.
- **§2.3, the 8 500 kg weight:** the importer did not declare it. It was **written on the verification body's report**.
- **§2.5, false declaration:** the 2019 code sets the fine **equal to** the value of the goods (art. 467) and up to triple in some cases (art. 468), so « jusqu'à » understates it. WEAK for 2026.
- **§2.5, BESC:** it means « Bordereau **électronique** de suivi des cargaisons ». The penalty amount is not published (VERIFIED).
- **§2.7, broker fees paid through CAMCIS since 2025:** press only (bougna.net, 09/06/2025). Status: WEAK.
- **§5, expat.com quotes:** they exist, from about 2013–2014, on a used vehicle and an express parcel. The courier in that thread is named, so no carrier brand on screen. The cameroun24 quote and the union president's quote are not verified. Status: WEAK.

## 2. Risky or forbidden for a Bonzini video

- **Hook 1**, « Combien de trop ? », assumes the viewer overpaid. That breaks the rule against presenting « vous payez trop » as a general truth. Use « Vous avez payé la douane. Mais combien deviez-vous vraiment ? »
- **Hook 2**, « Le prix de votre douane se décide avant de payer… », is wrong: customs sets the amount at declaration. Use « Votre douane, estimez-la avant de payer votre fournisseur. »
- **Hook 5**, « Un papier oublié… et la valeur triple », turns one internal case into a rule. Only the anonymised « environ trois fois » wording is allowed.
- **Hook 10** suggests paying less. Keep it only if « Ni plus, ni moins » follows.
- **Invented quotes** « La douane m'a tué » and « Ils ont gonflé » accuse customs or freight forwarders. Forbidden.
- **Banned words:**
  - « 11 jours gratuits » → « 11 jours de franchise »
  - « le seul vrai levier » in §1: avoid « seul » anywhere near Bonzini
- **Third-party branding:**
  - The voice-note visual must have no WhatsApp logo or interface.
  - No GUCE or SIMPA name, slogan or logo.
  - No Facebook, TikTok or Mobile Money logos.
- **LF2026 fines:** never tie them to Bonzini or to paying suppliers, because of art. 13 (hawala) and the currency clause in art. 14.3.
- **Rows 21, 22, 18, 28, 29** and the bribery figures from the survey stay forbidden, as do article numbers, per-product rates and real case amounts.
- Bonzini is not a customs broker, there is no refund promise, and the URL can appear only after a real live test. Otherwise use « Bientôt ». Always add « Estimation · à faire confirmer par un commissionnaire agréé ».

## 3. The 5 strongest SAFE facts for the teaser

1. **China is Cameroon's first supplier: 22.2 % of imports in 2024** (INS, official).
2. **VAT at import: 19.25 % at the standard rate, on the full amount, duty included** (CGI 2026).
3. **The customs duty runs from 0 to 40 % depending on your product's code.** The new common tariff has applied since 01/01/2026 (CNCC). Show the same chair labelled MEUBLE / SIÈGE, with no amounts.
4. **Douala port: 11 days of free period counted from unloading.** The official customs study names late payment of duties after the bill is calculated as a cause of delay (two sources; say « 11 jours », never « gratuits »).
5. **When the import declaration and the value report are required, leaving them out costs a fine of 25 % of the taxable value** (2026 finance law; always keep « quand ils sont obligatoires »).
