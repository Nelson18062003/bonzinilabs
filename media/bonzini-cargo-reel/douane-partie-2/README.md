# « DOUANE · Apprends à faire — Partie 2 » : « La tontine de Christelle » (3 min 37)

Part 2 of the customs series, after « Le premier conteneur de Junior » (Part 1). It covers the four topics Part 1
announced: **the taxes, the conformity checks, the simulation and the transitaire**.

**Owner feedback that shaped it.** The first art direction (paper cut-outs over photos) was rejected:
« le graphisme est horrible », « ce sont juste les photos, pourquoi tu n'as pas animé ça en dessin animé ». The owner asked for a story about young
African traders (« commerçants et commerçantes »), drawn like a cartoon and inspired by *Aya de Yopougon*. Part 2 is
therefore an **animated comic (BD animée)**:
- warm ink-and-gouache illustrations;
- characters that are articulated puppets — they breathe, talk, gesture, walk and hop;
- comic balloons, hand-lettered documents that write themselves on the voice;
- the narrator's yellow récitatif.

**The story.** On a Saturday night at the maquis in Mboppi, the band's tontine falls on Christelle. She sells hair
extensions and wants to pay for her first container from China before the holidays. Tantine Mireille keeps the envelope: « Pas de note de
douane, pas d'enveloppe ! ». Christelle has one week to make the « vraie note », and the film follows it day by day:
- **Monday (the taxes):** the duty by code, then excise for some products (hair extensions yes, new sneakers no), then VAT 19.25 % on the whole, duty included, and the small taxes. Under the real regime, VAT and the précompte come back; under the IGS, count them in your price.
- **Tuesday (conformity):** above 2 million F FOB, own container, two checks are prepared before departure (value and quality). Every paper has its moment; forgetting them costs a fine of 25 %; splitting an order to stay under the threshold is forbidden.
- **Wednesday (the simulation):** Nadège estimates the bill on the proforma in four steps, and SIMPA is open to everyone. The ESTIMATION stamp lands: « l'outil estime, le commissionnaire agréé déclare, la douane vérifie et calcule ».
- **Thursday:** Boris brings his « gars », the Roi du forfait, a comic, likeable, unlicensed fixer. Christelle asks for his licence: « Un nom sur une enseigne ne prouve rien. L'agrément, oui. »
- **Friday:** at Madame Ekambi's, a licensed customs broker. Her quote separates the State's share from her official fees. You pay the State through an official channel, never a person, against an electronic receipt. « Le transitaire signe. Mais la marchandise, c'est la vôtre. »
- **Saturday night:** Tantine hands over the envelope, Christelle sets the customs share aside, and Boris finally opens his box: an empty frame « pour ta première quittance ».

Later, customs calculates the bill, and there is no bad surprise. The film ends on Bonzini (« où vous réglez vos fournisseurs en francs
CFA »), the in-app estimate « en quelques questions, avant de payer votre fournisseur », a comment prompt and the
signature « Payez le juste droit. Ni plus, ni moins. »

Facts come only from `data/research_p2.md` (verified fact sheet, §5 = the only figures allowed on screen).
The shot list with all review corrections is `data/shots_final.md`. The cast and story notes are in `data/story_brief.md`, the art bible in `data/art_bible.md`.

## How it is built
- **Engine** (`overlay/`, Playwright + canvas, 1080×1920 @ 30 fps). The core is `kit.js`. The scene files are:
  - `08_puppet.js` — WebGL mesh-skinned puppets: bone weights, jaw and blink, breathing, talk gestures, hop, walk cycle, lighting grades;
  - `09_bd.js` — shots, cameras in stage units, transitions, ink strokes, balloons, stamps;
  - `10_plan.js` — voice-timeline helpers;
  - `11_rigs.js` — generated by `lib/gen/auto_rig.py` with MediaPipe Pose;
  - `12_props.js` — the recurring drawn props, the animated margouillat, and the two procedural top-down backgrounds;
  - `05_tag.js`, `90_recit.js` — series tag and narrator captions;
  - **one file per day of the story**: `21_samedi` (P1–P3), `22_lundi` (P4–P8), `23_mardi` (P9–P11), `24_mercredi` (P12–P14), `25_jeudi` (P15–P17), `26_vendredi` (P18–P22), `27_samedi_soir` (P23–P25), `28_plus_tard` (P26–P28).

  `SCENE_GUIDE_P2.md` is the animators' guide. All times come from the voice (`tw/te/ss/se/shotStart`), never hard-coded seconds.
- **Pictures** (not versioned, like Part 1's photos; see `CREDITS.md`):
  - **Canva AI** in the owner's account: the character sheets of Junior and Christelle, the maquis at night, the office, the sneaker stall and the street.
  - The people in the Canva scenes were removed by local inpainting (`lib/gen/inpaint.py`, masks in `data/gen/`), so the animated puppets can stand there.
  - **Secondary cast** (Nadège, Boris, Tantine Mireille, Madame Ekambi, le Roi du forfait) generated locally with stable-diffusion.cpp + DreamShaper XL Lightning, img2img from the Canva sheets so the style matches (`data/gen/batch3.json`).
  - The cast was cut out with rembg (isnet-anime) and upscaled ×2 with Real-ESRGAN.
  - Nadège's dress was recoloured red; Boris's box was drawn in.
  - Poses with high slits were discarded.
- **Voice**: Kyutai TTS 1.6B, voice `unmute-prod-website/developpeuse-3` (same voice as the series). Each take was checked by ASR (faster-whisper large-v3), and alternate takes were kept for S5, S6, S11, S21 and S25 (`take` in `data/script.json`). Real timeline: `data/timeline.json` (217.25 s).
- **Sound**: a makossa groove whose level follows the week (`lib/makossa.py`, `lib/cues.py`), ambience beds per place (`lib/amb.py`), and BD foley generated from the render itself.
  - `render.mjs --dump-cues` records balloon pops, page turns, slides, captions and pen writing.
  - `--dump-shakes` records stamps and impacts.
  - Mix −14 LUFS, voice ≈ 13 LU over the bed (`lib/audio.py`).

## Rebuild
```bash
python3 lib/tts_kyutai.py --seed 1          # voice takes → audio/vo/
python3 lib/build_timeline.py               # data/timeline.json + audio/voice.wav
cd overlay && node render.mjs --out ../out/frames --pages 4 --jpg --dump-cues ../data/cues_auto.json --dump-shakes ../data/shakes.json
cd .. && python3 lib/audio.py && bash lib/encode.sh
```
The pictures must be placed in `assets/img/bg/*.png` and `assets/img/cast/*.png` (keys listed in `SCENE_GUIDE_P2.md`;
fonts in `assets/fonts/`).

## Rules kept (see the repo's media guidelines and `data/shots_final.md`)
- No carrier brand, no logo or emblem except Bonzini. Sneaker logos were painted out.
- No customs officer and no money changing hands. The Roi du forfait stays likeable.
- Modest outfits; no real person and no real document.
- Only the figures of `research_p2.md` §5.
- Never « transfert d'argent » or « envoyer ».
- The Bonzini app never appears next to the payment of Christelle's supplier.
